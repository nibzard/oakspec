Notes and results from the OAK project.

  • 2026-11-24Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, and Pi on one repo: where OAK v0.5 beat plain Markdown
    We ran four coding agents on a fork of pallets/itsdangerous to test OAK v0.5 against plain Markdown notes. All four passed setup and tests. OAK cut Git merge conflicts to 0 of 9 and blocked 3 of 3 silent file overwrites outside Git.
  • 2026-11-19Your agent says it checked your machine. It did not.
    Agents in sandboxes check their own environment and report the result as a fact about your laptop. In our pilot, three short reading rules cut these false verifications from 11 to 14 of every 30 runs down to 0 to 2. A location field alone did much less.
  • 2026-11-19What agents need to remember
    OAK gives project knowledge a shared home. Our handoff tests helped us decide what belongs in the spec and what agents can work out for themselves.
